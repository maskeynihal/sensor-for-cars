# Idea: HJ60 coolant temperature from the OEM sender

- Date: 2026-11-01
- Status: Building -> [projects/hj60_coolant](../../projects/hj60_coolant/)
- Board: ESP32 DevKit V1 (WROOM-32)

## Problem

The HJ60's 2H diesel only has a bimetal dash gauge for coolant temperature. The head cracks if it overheats. We want a
number, an alarm the driver can't miss, and later a log and adjustable limits, without breaking the factory gauge.

## Sketch of a solution

- Inputs: the factory temperature sender, tapped ratiometrically at the gauge (feed E + sender F). Later a PTFE DS18B20
  on the upper hose as a cross-check.
- Outputs: 1.3" SH1106 OLED (static readout), active buzzer through a transistor.
- Power: 24 V ignition-switched, fused, TVS, ≥60 V-input buck to 5 V.
- Where it mounts: cabin, near the cluster.

## Open questions

- Rg on this cluster, the regulator waveform, the ground offset, the full sender curve (report section 6, phase M).
- Thresholds: 100 °C warn / 105 °C alarm to start. Confirm with the owner's experience of the truck.
- Coolant-loss detection: rate of change, second sensor, or level probe?
- Logging medium: microSD with hold-up, or NVS summaries.
